# Demographic Protocol Applicability Policy

This policy applies to every open-source protocol batch.

1. Pregnancy-specific protocols are Female-only. Male variants must not be generated.
2. Pediatric-specific protocols are Child-only and must respect the source-supported minimum and maximum ages. Adult variants must not be generated.
3. Adult-specific protocols are Adult-only. Child variants must not be generated by relabeling adult content.
4. Mixed-age protocols may have Adult and Child variants only when the cited source supports both populations.
5. A pregnancy assessment may appear in an applicable Adult Female protocol for routing or treatment planning, but it must not appear in a Male protocol.
6. Pediatric-only thresholds and wording must not remain in Adult protocol questions.
7. Sex-specific or age-specific clinical differences must be supported by cited evidence. When no difference is supported, the manifest must say so rather than inventing one.
8. Clinical applicability takes precedence over producing a fixed number of demographic variants.

Every batch validator must check these rules before the batch is considered structurally complete.
